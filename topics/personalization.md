# Personalization

Personalization is not merely a larger chat history. It is controlled adaptation to a person's preferences, recurring context, expertise, and goals.

## Design constraints

- Separate observed facts from inferred preferences.
- Record confidence, provenance, consent, and expiry.
- Let users inspect, correct, export, and delete memories.
- Evaluate usefulness alongside privacy, stereotyping, over-personalization, and stale assumptions.

Use LaMP, PrefEval, and PERSONAMEM to compare profile-conditioned tasks, preference following, and long-term dialogue memory.
