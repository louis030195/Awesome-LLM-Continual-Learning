# Evaluation and Benchmarks

Continual systems should be measured over a sequence, not at one final snapshot.

## Minimum scorecard

- Acquisition and immediate task utility
- Retention and catastrophic forgetting
- Backward and forward transfer
- Memory precision, recall, conflict resolution, and abstention
- Latency, token/storage cost, and update cost
- Privacy leakage, deletion compliance, and adversarial robustness

LongMemEval, LoCoMo, MemoryAgentBench, LaMP, PrefEval, TiC-LM, RECALL, and SkillLearnBench cover complementary slices; no single benchmark establishes general continual learning.
